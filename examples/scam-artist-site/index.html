Drag structures into your rooms and make every usable tile count. Leave room for later rearrangement as stronger objects become available.

Customers move through the apartment and interact with the money-making setups you have placed, turning room planning into income.

Decorations and renovations are more than visual flair. Their effects can improve the efficiency of your growing operation.

TIP 01

Use space carefully

Placement is part of the strategy. Keep enough room to reorganize when stronger structures and decorations unlock.

TIP 02

Read decoration effects

Decorations are not cosmetic only. Treat useful effects like upgrades and combine them with your room plan.

TIP 03

Use Scam Society research

Trade the required documents for research points when Scam Society opens up, then use that progression to unlock more elaborate scams.

Controls

  • Drag and drop objects to place them.
  • Hold a placed object to rearrange it.
  • Drag the view to move the camera.
  • On mobile, use two fingers to move the camera.

FAQ

Before You Play

Can I play Scam Artist directly in my browser?

Yes. The current prototype includes an HTML5 browser build on itch.io, and the player above loads that hosted build.

Do I need to download anything?

No for the browser prototype. The Steam version is currently coming soon and lists Windows system requirements.

Does the game work on mobile?

The developer describes the interaction system as touch-friendly. Drag-and-drop is the core input and camera movement uses two fingers on mobile.

Is the full Steam game released?

Not yet. The Steam listing currently shows the full game as coming soon.

Who made Scam Artist?

The game is developed and published by greenpixels.
