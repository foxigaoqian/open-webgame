Beginner Playbook

TIP 01

Use space carefully

Placement is part of the strategy. Leave yourself enough room to reorganize as stronger objects and decorations unlock.

TIP 02

Read decoration effects

Decorations are not cosmetic only. Their effects can materially change your growth, so treat them like upgrades.

TIP 03

Push toward automation

The current prototype includes automated money collection systems that reduce repetitive clicking later in the run.

Controls

  • Drag and drop objects to place them.
  • Hold a placed object to rearrange it.
  • Drag the view to move the camera.
  • On mobile, use two fingers to move the camera.

FAQ

Before You Play

Can I play Scam Artist directly in my browser?

Yes. The current prototype includes an HTML5 browser build on itch.io, and the player above loads that hosted build.

Do I need to download anything?

No for the browser prototype. Windows and Linux builds also exist for players who prefer a desktop version.

Does the game work on mobile?

The developer describes the interaction system as touch-friendly. Drag-and-drop is the core input and camera movement uses two fingers on mobile.

Is the full Steam game released?

Not yet. The Steam listing currently shows the full game as coming soon.

Who made Scam Artist?

The game is developed and published by greenpixels.
