Fields of Mistria Romance & Marriage Guide

Version 1.0 completes the relationship arc with 10-heart proposal events, engagement, weddings, marriage and optional children. Here is the verified structure without inventing secret requirements.

How many marriage candidates?

The current official game description lists 12 marriage candidates. Romance develops through the broader relationship system: regular interaction, heart progression and character events. Rather than treating romance as a separate minigame, it fits into the same town routine as requests, festivals and everyday conversations.

The 10-heart stage

Version 1.0 raises the relationship cap to 10 hearts and adds 10-heart proposal events. If you are coming from an older save, this is the major relationship milestone that did not exist in the earlier Early Access structure.

NPC Studio's 1.0 notes explain that the Engagement Ring becomes craftable at the Forge after reaching 10 hearts with a character you are dating. Elsie delivers the recipe the next morning. The 10-heart event can also be retriggered with an Engagement Ring before you commit to a proposal, giving players room to experience the event without immediately locking the choice.

Proposal and engagement

Once you decide who to marry, the Engagement Ring is the proposal item. The important practical advice is simple: do not rush the final commitment just because the item is available. Finish any relationship events or role-playing goals you care about first, especially if you have been dating multiple characters.

The 1.0 update also adds relationship-status behavior for players dating multiple characters at engagement time. Treat engagement as the point where your social save state becomes more permanent.

Wedding customization

The full release includes wedding events and several choices around the ceremony. Official notes mention customizable wedding flowers, cake and meal. These details make the wedding more than a single dialogue confirmation and are worth preparing for if your save is relationship-focused.

Children and family planning

Marriage does not automatically force children. NPC Studio's 1.0 notes describe a Mystical Feather obtained after marriage for optional family planning. That means players can remain married without immediately pursuing the child system.

Relationship strategy

The efficient route is not necessarily to spam one interaction while ignoring the rest of town life. Fold relationship progress into existing travel: speak with important villagers while shopping, turning in requests or moving between farm and town. Festivals and character events are also part of the social rhythm, so calendar awareness matters.

Fact check: this page describes the relationship framework confirmed in official 1.0 notes. It does not invent candidate preferences or gift values that were not verified for this build.
