Fields of Mistria Beginner Guide

The opening is easier when you treat farming, town restoration, relationships and gathering as one connected progression loop instead of trying to max one system immediately.

Core idea: finish each day with more options for tomorrow. Money matters, but so do unlocks, relationships, materials and town progress.

First-day priorities

Clear only the farm space you actually need. Early stamina is valuable, and spending a whole day removing every branch or rock delays learning the map, meeting villagers and finding the town's useful locations. Establish a manageable crop area, then use the rest of the day to understand where shops, requests and gathering routes fit into your schedule.

Keep basic materials instead of selling everything. Wood, stone and other gathered resources often become more valuable when they enable crafting or repair progress. A clean inventory is nice, but an empty stockpile can force you to repeat work later.

Build a repeatable daily loop

A practical routine is: check crops and animals first, identify any time-sensitive request or relationship goal, choose one main stamina activity, and leave enough time to gather or speak with villagers on the way home. The important part is not the exact order; it is avoiding days where all stamina disappears before you decide what the day was supposed to accomplish.

Spend stamina with a target

Before mining, ask what ore, artifact, quest step or upgrade you need. Before clearing a large farm section, ask what you will build there. Before planting many crops, consider whether watering them will consume too much of tomorrow. This simple target-based approach keeps expansion from creating chores faster than it creates progress.

Do not ignore town progression

Fields of Mistria's identity is broader than farm profit. Requests, Town Repair quests, Renown, museum donations and relationships all move the wider game forward. Version 1.0 makes the Town Repair questline completable and raises Town Renown to 100, so long-term town activity is not side filler; it is a major progression track.

Early money without tunnel vision

Crop income is dependable, but a beginner should avoid treating every item as instant cash. Fishing, mining, gathering and ranching feed several systems at once. Sell enough to finance the next useful step, while retaining materials likely to unlock crafting, requests or collections.

What to learn next

Once your routine feels stable, decide which long-term system you want to push: deeper mine progress, more developed ranching, stronger town relationships, romance, museum completion or farm optimization. The game supports breadth, but choosing a temporary focus gives your days purpose.
