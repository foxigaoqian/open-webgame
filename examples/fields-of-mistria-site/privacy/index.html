Privacy Policy

A plain-language privacy note for this independent Fields of Mistria guide.

Site hosting

This site is published as static web content. Like most web hosting services, the hosting platform may process standard technical request information such as IP addresses, browser information, timestamps and requested URLs for security, reliability and operational purposes.

No account system

This Wiki does not provide its own player account, login, save-file upload, payment form or game-hosting service. You do not need to submit personal profile information to read the guides.

External links

Links to Steam, NPC Studio and other official destinations leave this site. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and may set cookies or collect information according to their own terms. Opening an external link is not the same as submitting information to this Wiki.

Game purchases

Any purchase of Fields of Mistria takes place on an external official storefront such as Steam. This independent Wiki does not process payment card data or receive your Steam login credentials.

Policy updates

This page may be revised if the site's technical setup changes. Material changes should be reflected here rather than silently implying privacy practices that are no longer accurate.
