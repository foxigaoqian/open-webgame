Fields of Mistria 1.0 Update Guide

The August 5, 2026 full release closes major progression arcs, completes relationship systems and turns the former Early Access roadmap into a 1.0 game.

Current patch: v1.0.2, published August 18, 2026. The 1.0 feature list below comes from NPC Studio's official full-release patch notes.

Big 1.0 milestones

The Town Repair Questline can now be completed, Town Renown rises to 100, the Museum can be completed and a Mistril Town Rank event extends civic progression. These changes matter to long-running saves because town restoration now has a true end state rather than stopping at an Early Access ceiling.

Steam achievements also arrived with retroactive unlock behavior for existing saves. That means players returning to a developed farm can receive credit for qualifying progress instead of repeating the entire game solely for achievements.

Relationships, marriage and children

Version 1.0 raises the heart cap to 10 and adds 10-heart proposal events. The Engagement Ring becomes craftable after reaching 10 hearts with a character you are dating, with Elsie delivering the recipe on the following morning. Wedding events and marriage complete the relationship path, and wedding flowers, cake and meal can be customized.

After marriage, the Mystical Feather supports optional family planning and the child system. NPC Studio also added relationship-status settings for engagement situations involving multiple dating relationships.

New town progression

The full release expands the Saturday Market with a Plaza quest and adds a Repair Bell Tower quest. Stillwell and Zorel join the Saturday Market. Stillwell introduces Mission Quests through a Mission Board, while Zorel sells Crystal Resonators and Song Crystals for music customization on the farm and in the player's home.

Perks and customization

Seven new perks arrive with 1.0, alongside additional customization. Fully upgraded homes can change exterior style through the Carpenter's Shop. Character customization gains rotation, while graphics settings and other quality-of-life changes make the game easier to tune.

Localization

The 1.0 notes introduce beta localization for Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Spanish. NPC Studio has continued localization work in follow-up patches rather than treating the launch translations as frozen.

Performance work

NPC Studio reports further 1.0 performance improvements, including more than 50% lower VRAM usage. Patch v1.0.2 continues that work by improving performance when AMD or Nvidia overlays are enabled.

What v1.0.2 fixes

Patch v1.0.2 adds new hairstyles and additional translations while addressing many post-launch problems. One of the most visible fixes is the Shooting Star Festival softlock. The patch also resolves several wedding, child, mount, collision and rare crash edge cases.
