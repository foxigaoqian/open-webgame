What is Fields of Mistria?

Farm life, town restoration and returning magic

Mistria is a village between forest and sea recovering from an earthquake. The player receives an overgrown homestead, helps repair the town and gradually discovers why magic is returning to the land.

🌾

Farming & customization

Grow crops, shape the farm, customize your character and home, and adapt your routine to changing seasons.

🎣

Fishing, mining & crafting

Gather resources through fishing and the mines, then turn materials into upgrades, collections and useful crafted items.

✨

Magic

Magic expands both farm and adventure progression, giving the game a fantasy layer beyond ordinary tool upgrades.

💗

Romance & family

The current game has 12 marriage candidates; 1.0 completed proposal, wedding, marriage and children systems.

🐮

Animals

Raise livestock, care for them over time and pursue rare color variants as a longer-term ranching goal.

🏘️

Restore Mistria

Requests, Town Repair quests, Renown, the Museum and villager stories connect personal farm progress to the town itself.

Release state

Confirmed vs. unknown

A useful Wiki shows where official information stops.

Confirmed

  • Full 1.0 PC release launched August 5, 2026.
  • Windows and Linux / SteamOS requirements appear on Steam.
  • The official FAQ references Steam Deck compatibility.
  • Marriage, children and 10-heart proposal events are available in 1.0.
  • v1.0.2 is the latest official patch checked on August 18, 2026.

Do not assume

  • Console timing is not confirmed; NPC Studio's press kit lists console as TBA.
  • No verified official browser / HTML5 version is available.
  • Future post-1.0 features are not facts until NPC Studio announces them.

Quick answers

Fields of Mistria FAQ

Answers stay tied to official sources.

Is Fields of Mistria fully released?

Yes. Version 1.0 launched August 5, 2026, exactly two years after the August 5, 2024 Early Access launch.

What is the latest version?

NPC Studio published v1.0.2 on August 18, 2026. This site treats that as a volatile fact and re-checks it frequently.

Can I play Fields of Mistria in a browser?

No verified official browser build is available. The game is distributed as a native Steam title.

Does Fields of Mistria have marriage?

Yes. Version 1.0 added proposal events, engagement, weddings, marriage and children.

How many marriage candidates are there?

The current official Steam description says there are 12 marriage candidates.

Is it on console?

No console release date is confirmed in the official press kit, which currently lists console as TBA.
