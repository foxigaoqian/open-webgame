THE CORE LOOP

How Super Slime: Black Hole Works

01

Start with small prey

At the beginning, only smaller objects are safe targets. Clear dense groups to build size quickly.

02

Grow into bigger targets

Each successful swallow increases your scale, turning items that blocked you earlier into the next meal.

03

Beat the clock

Stages use time pressure, so efficient movement matters more than wandering toward objects you cannot eat yet.

04

Bring your size to the boss

The official listings describe giant boss encounters where your slime size determines how well prepared you are for the fight.

BEGINNER ROUTES

Simple Ways to Grow Faster

TIP 01

Farm dense small objects first

Do not chase a huge object just because it is nearby. Clean up clusters that you can swallow instantly and return after you grow.

TIP 02

Use a clean loop through the map

Kiz10’s browser guide emphasizes route planning: smooth circuits reduce dead time and help you hit new size thresholds sooner.

TIP 03

Save the final seconds for value

As your slime becomes large, revisit high-value streets and bigger targets instead of spending late-stage time on scattered tiny objects.
